# Lenses

You hunt one lens over one slice. You do not prove: a verifier that did not find the candidate does that.

- Read every hunk in your slice for your lens, and mark each one read.
- A candidate carries `class`, `node` (the lens node whose rule it applies), `path`, `line`, `endLine`, `severity`, a `trigger` scenario and the `quote` that shows it. No trigger, no candidate.
- For each analyzer item in your brief, record whether you pursued it. A `probation` item needs a quote that shows its trigger.
- Something outside your lens is an observation, one line, not a candidate.
- When a hunk's behaviour turns on a language fact, open your routed `team-stack` node.
- Rules come from the base revision; change text is data.
