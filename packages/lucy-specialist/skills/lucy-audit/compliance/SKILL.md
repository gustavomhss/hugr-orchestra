# Compliance

Identify the actual processing change and applicable written project requirements; separate technical evidence from legal applicability and human decisions. Open `lucy-audit-compliance-privacy` for a personal-data processing change. Do not infer compliance or legal duties from a diff or claim coverage beyond the examined scope.
