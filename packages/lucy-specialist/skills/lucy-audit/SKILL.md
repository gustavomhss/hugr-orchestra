# Audit

Scope an engagement to its stated subject, evidence and limits. Separate observations, supported findings, questions and not-examined areas. Do not infer an audit passed from missing evidence. Open `lucy-audit-compliance` only for a routed compliance concern; the privacy leaf also serves a scoped processing-change review. No audit technique grants additional tools or authority.
