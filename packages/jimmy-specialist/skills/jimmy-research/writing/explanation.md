# Write reasons, mechanisms and bounded conclusions

Read for genre `explanation`: journeys, state/lifecycle, causal investigation, analytical argument, change consequence or feasibility. The reader needs the bridge from evidence to conclusion, not a chronological research diary.

## Lead with the mechanism and its limit

State the supported central answer and condition: what controls the outcome, which guarantee applies, where ownership moves or which requirement is feasible. If the central cause is unresolved, lead with the narrowed possibilities and exact discriminator rather than manufacturing a confident headline.

An explanation of source behavior can be sufficient without runtime activation, provided it says so. A causal claim requires evidence appropriate to the causal question; a plausible branch does not establish a production incident's cause.

## Order necessary reasons

Organize a journey as input, interpretation, state/effect and result with the ownership/exception at each relevant seam. Include only steps needed for the question. A cancellation or error path that changes the conclusion belongs in the main argument, not a decorative appendix.

Organize an analytical explanation by premises and dependencies. Explain how each supported premise leads to the next claim; identify assumptions at the point they matter. Do not present two extracts followed by a conclusion whose bridge remains unstated.

Organize an investigation around competing predictions and the first evidenced divergence. Show why the observations discriminate one explanation, what contrary case narrows it and what remains unresolved. Timestamp correlation or a recent commit alone is not enough.

For feasibility, connect each decisive requirement to mechanism and transfer conditions. Distinguish documented fit, static implementation compatibility and observed target behavior. A proposed probe is next work, not a feasibility result.

## Use support and opposition together

Put evidence beside the claim it supports and explain its scope. Where sources conflict, compare versions, contexts or definitions before asserting contradiction. Preserve a live rival if evidence cannot distinguish it. A decisive qualification belongs in the summary and its dependent paragraphs.

A useful hypothetical argument is: “Admission can reconcile repeated input locally; publication follows commit, and no examined destination contract establishes deduplication. This leaves external uniqueness unresolved. Delivery identity and destination receipts would distinguish republication from redelivery.” It does not claim either event was observed.

## Make the consequence actionable

Explain what this means for the reader's question: correct starting seam, guarantee to preserve, bounded impact, conditional choice or smallest next proof. Recommendations go to Maestro and qualified owners; avoid architectural approval or a product implementation plan beyond the evidenced need.
