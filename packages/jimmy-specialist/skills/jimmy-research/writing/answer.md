# Write a direct, qualified answer

Read for genre `answer`: a simple applicable fact, exact location, narrow claim audit or short supported conclusion. Do not replace a multi-part mechanism or comparison with an unexplained verdict; use the relevant genre instead.

## Answer the wording

Begin with the requested fact or entry and its decisive condition. A location answer explains why that entry is effective, not merely where its name appears. A versioned rule states the applicable version and exception, not a universal paraphrase. If the premise is false, correct it with the source before suggesting further work.

Supply the minimum explanation that makes the answer usable. For “where is this selected?”, identify the selector and body relation. For “does this support retry?”, distinguish the stage covered by the guarantee. Do not answer a neighboring question because its source is easier to find.

## Keep evidence proportional

Retain the applicable native source receipt and exact locator, then cite/attribute the decisive passage. A one-source answer can remain a paragraph in the final card; it does not need a separately recorded question/finding, bibliography document, review ceremony or Memory lesson.

If the brief promises a standalone answer document, publish it natively with actual citation-map references. Do not invent a local output path or use a URL as a `SourceRef`. Source, revision and qualification should be legible to the reader even when exact receipt objects are separately carried in the card.

## Make uncertainty specific

State what the source establishes and what the question additionally requires. For example, documentation can establish an official default while an unknown local override prevents a claim about the running process. Keep that limit in the answer sentence, not only in a footnote.

When essential evidence is unavailable, do not write only “cannot answer”. Give the supported part, exact blocker and smallest missing observation with its responsible executor. Preserve permission denial/verification holds verbatim; do not fill the gap with an assumed runtime or a different unauthorized route.

## Finish without padding

Remove source-by-source summaries, redundant headings and generic “further research” endings. Include a next action only when unresolved evidence or an actual reader need warrants it. The answer is complete when the exact bounded question and material exception are supported, not when it reaches a length quota.
