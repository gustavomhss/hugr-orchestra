# Write an opportunity as a falsifiable value argument

Read for genre `opportunity` after [opportunity discovery](../methods/opportunities.md). The document should make useful next validation possible; it should not sell an unsupported roadmap or present novelty as demand.

## Anchor value in a need

Lead with the bounded opportunity hypothesis and decisive condition. Explain whose workflow/outcome is affected and what evidence establishes the problem. Separate observed friction, stakeholder reports and speculative interest. If need evidence is absent, say the hypothesis is mechanism-led and demand remains unknown.

Describe what people use now, including workarounds and no change. Explain the evidenced reason alternatives leave the unmet part: access, resource limits, workflow, reliability or another scoped condition. A category missing from searched results is not an empty market. A current alternative that solves the problem is material contrary evidence.

## Build the mechanism-to-value bridge

Connect the proposed mechanism to the unmet part: why might it change the desired outcome, under which conditions, and what source supports that bridge? A benchmark advantage can support a mechanism hypothesis without proving local benefit, customer adoption or willingness to pay.

Keep source conditions visible: population, workload, version, operational burden, transfer costs and external dependencies when material. A new combination is interesting only if it addresses an evidenced need better than the actual baseline under those conditions.

The opportunity's argument should read as a chain: need evidence → limits of current alternatives → promising mechanism → conditional value → smallest test. Each link needs support or an explicit hypothesis, not an empty section label. Several attractive ideas without that chain are not a research result.

## Expose the weak assumption

Identify the assumption most likely to overturn the value claim. Preserve contrary evidence of low need, an adequate incumbent, incompatible environment or excessive cost. Do not bury it in generic risks after an unconditional recommendation.

Propose the smallest validation that tests that assumption, with specific inputs/population/context, outcome measure, baseline and discard criterion. Name who can perform it through Maestro. A useful result might be an observation or qualified scratch probe; this role does not contact users, delegate, edit product or run arbitrary experiments to manufacture demand evidence.

Discard criteria matter as much as success criteria. Explain what finding would make the opportunity not worth pursuing or send it back to framing. A conditional negative result can save more work than an inflated opportunity list.

## Close with honest status

Distinguish researched gap, value hypothesis, proposed validation and observed validation result. Do not invent market-size scores, admission receipts, independent acceptance or product priority. Scope novelty/gap claims to examined evidence and calibrated coverage.

Check cohesive order and support with [synthesis](synthesis.md). Publish promised text natively with the exact findings/citation map, then use [closure](../../jimmy-discovery/references/closure.md). The conclusion enables a precise next decision while keeping validated demand, architecture approval and implementation separate.
