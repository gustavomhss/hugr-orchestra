# Write a thematic evidence review

Read for genre `evidence-review`, after selecting the honest [review method](../methods/evidence-literature.md). A review synthesizes what evidence means for the question; it is not an annotated bibliography in source order.

## State what was reviewed

Lead with the central answer and its decisive applicability/uncertainty limit. State the bounded question, actual design—narrative, scoping/mapping, systematic or rapid—and source/selection reach. Deep describes effort, not review design. Shortened steps belong in a rapid review's method and conclusion limits.

Explain the search/eligibility choices that matter to the coverage claim. Distinguish planned from executed steps, amendments with reasons, inaccessible plausible evidence and deliberate exclusions. Preserve actual queries/date/filter/source details proportionally in the review or an admitted companion document. Do not invent PRISMA flow counts or imply database coverage from ordinary web fetches.

## Organize by questions and disagreements

Group evidence around mechanisms, outcomes, populations, methods or disputes relevant to the reader. Start each section with the thematic proposition, then show which studies support, qualify or contradict it and why. Use individual study detail when design/context explains a difference, not because every paper needs its own paragraph.

Distinguish multiple reports of one study from independent evidence. Retain design, sample/context, relevant outcome/uncertainty and limitations for material conclusions. Abstract-only and secondary-source claims cannot be described as full-text reading. Missing evidence is not evidence of no effect.

For contradictory findings, inspect outcome definitions, context, comparison, study design and bias. Explain plausible condition-dependent reconciliation only when supported; otherwise preserve the conflict. Do not vote-count favorable papers, pool incompatible estimates or turn a map of gaps into an efficacy verdict.

## Connect evidence to implications

State what the body of examined evidence supports and where the inference reaches beyond it. A limitation that changes transfer belongs alongside that implication and in the summary. Keep an agenda tied to exact gaps: missing population, unresolved mechanism, inaccessible full text or target-specific proof, not generic “more research”.

Critical appraisal is appropriate to the actual question/design, not an invented quality score. PRISMA 2020 supports reporting, not a correctness certificate. [Study reading](../references/study-reading.md) provides metric and ablation checks before comparative claims.

## Audit and publish

Check that each material clause is supported by applicable captured evidence and that multi-source inference explains its bridge. Reverse-outline the thematic argument using [synthesis](synthesis.md); a polished bibliography cannot repair missing premises.

Publish exact text, finding revisions and admitted citation map with native [record operations](../../jimmy-discovery/references/record-tools.md), including a promised methods companion before the final card. Close with supported answer, contradictions, limits and precise next proof using [closure](../../jimmy-discovery/references/closure.md). Name incomplete method/access honestly rather than claiming exhaustive review.
