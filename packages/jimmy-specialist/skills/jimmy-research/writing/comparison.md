# Write a comparison that explains a choice

Read for genre `comparison`: alternatives, a baseline-relative claim or a feasibility decision. The matrix is supporting evidence; the argument explains which difference matters under which conditions.

## Put the decision first

Lead with the conditional recommendation, a justified tie, retention of the current baseline or the proof that prevents a choice. Preserve the version/environment/preference that could reverse it. Do not announce an unconditional winner whose limitations appear only later.

Explain the actual baseline and decision criteria before presenting candidate detail. Use concrete requirements and eliminating constraints. If a missing stakeholder preference determines the outcome, expose that trade-off for Maestro rather than inventing weights.

## Build comparable cells

Choose only criteria that can change the answer. A cell contains a supported property or observation with its conditions and citation, not a marketing adjective. Distinguish documented, implemented and observed evidence. Include actual versions, workload/data and platform where results depend on them.

Represent “not examined”, “source does not address”, “unavailable”, “contradicted” and “supported” distinctly. An empty cell is not evidence of missing functionality. Do not combine headline measurements from incompatible benchmarks/settings or call mirrors independent studies.

Keep a matrix readable by moving detailed provenance into cited findings/documents, not by deleting decisive qualifiers. If a table makes comparison less clear, use ordered paragraphs around the shared criteria instead. No specific columns or score format are mandatory.

## Explain the synthesis

After the matrix, reason across criteria: why a requirement dominates, what costs transfer introduces and where an alternative has a material advantage. Include the no-change option. A feature advantage may not justify migration if integration/operational requirements remain unsupported.

Show material contrary evidence next to the affected conclusion. A recommendation can be “candidate A when this condition holds; otherwise retain the baseline until this probe resolves the boundary.” If all candidates fail a decisive criterion, state that rather than manufacturing a winner.

For empirical evidence, separate metric meaning, comparability and cost completeness using [study reading](../references/study-reading.md). Unknown telemetry stays unknown; no uncalibrated confidence or arbitrary weighted ranking replaces evidence.

## Close the decision honestly

Name the precise unresolved property and smallest proof, including expected discriminating outcomes and qualified executor when needed. Proposed installation, benchmark or implementation is not completed work. Product selection and architecture/security acceptance remain with their owners.

Check [synthesis](synthesis.md), publish promised native text with actual findings and citation map, then follow [closure](../../jimmy-discovery/references/closure.md). A comparison can be complete without a winner if the bounded question asks for differences; a requested choice remains partial when decisive evidence is unavailable.
