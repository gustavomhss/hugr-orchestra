# Compose an argument, not a search transcript

Read before composing any answer or document, then read its genre guide. These rules support both [discovery](../../jimmy-discovery/SKILL.md) and [research](../SKILL.md). They do not require a long document for a short question.

## Select and order

Reopen decisive support and contrary passages before writing. Select findings that answer the reader's question, not every source collected. State the central answer and the condition that would change it. If that sentence cannot be supported, narrow it or return to investigation.

Make a proportional argument outline: answer/condition, necessary reasons or mechanism, material contrary evidence, consequence or missing proof. A field map instead orders useful distinctions and their relationships; an evidence review orders the reader's questions and disagreements. Headings, matrices, timelines and diagrams are optional aids, not measures of completion.

Use normal technical prose in the recipient's language unless the mission specifies otherwise. A short answer can combine the entire argument in one paragraph. A deep document can open with a concise summary that preserves the decisive exception.

## Explain the bridge

Give each paragraph a job. State its proposition, bring in relevant evidence, then explain why the evidence supports that proposition under the conditions. A citation beside an inference does not supply its reasoning. Connect paragraphs by dependency or contrast, not “Source A says… Source B says…”.

Distinguish extraction, observation, inference, hypothesis and proposed action in wording. Exact quotations preserve the captured wording/context; translation or paraphrase is not an exact quote. Keep a qualifier next to the clause it limits. Material contrary evidence belongs in the argument, not only a closing caveat.

Hypothetical example: a local admission contract excludes external effects, while publisher source shows publication after commit. A valid synthesis is “The examined guarantee protects local admission; these sources do not establish destination uniqueness. Duplication still depends on publication retry and destination deduplication.” It is not “therefore retries duplicate effects and the queue must be replaced.” The latter adds unobserved behavior and an unsupported design decision.

## Check actual paragraph order

Reverse-outline the draft by stating each paragraph's actual function. Remove duplicated work, repair missing premises and reorder qualifications before consequences that depend on them. Check the headline/summary against the body: neither may promise more than its support.

Verify citations clause by clause in their applicable representations. Multi-source bridges need explanation; mirrors do not create independent corroboration. Reopen any source whose decisive passage was only previewed or summarized. A new claim discovered in writing returns to source/finding checks and material revision before publication.

## Choose the genre

Read [answer](answer.md) for a precise fact/location, [map](map.md) for connected terrain, [explanation](explanation.md) for mechanism/cause/feasibility, [comparison](comparison.md) for conditional choice, [evidence review](evidence-review.md) for literature, or [opportunity](opportunity.md) for a value hypothesis. Compose forms when the question needs it; do not force every response into six templates.

Publish promised documents with the exact [record input](../../jimmy-discovery/references/record-tools.md), admitted findings and citation map, then follow [closure](../../jimmy-discovery/references/closure.md). Formatting, admission and schema parsing establish different facts from source support or independent acceptance.
