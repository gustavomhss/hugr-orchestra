# Read scientific methods and ablations

Read before using a paper, empirical result, benchmark or ablation to support an analytical, comparative, evidence or applied claim. Do not transfer a headline result to this specialist or target environment without its conditions.

## Read the actual object

Identify paper version/date, representation, full-text versus abstract access, dataset/evaluator version, source-code pin and any disclosed artifacts. The same nominal paper version can differ across abstract/HTML or an implementation; preserve a discrepancy instead of selecting the convenient number.

Read the research question, methods, results, limitations and relevant appendices, not only the introduction's claim. Identify population/sampling, units of analysis, intervention, comparator, outcomes, oracle/judge, exclusions, missing trials and uncertainty. Cite the material actually read; unavailable supplemental content remains a limitation.

## Separate metrics and conclusions

Accuracy, precision, abstention, retrieval recall, citation support, fluency, latency and cost answer different questions. A correctly attributed claim can still be false or inapplicable. A function-location benchmark does not establish incident causality; persistence on a dialogue benchmark does not establish restart recovery of source artifacts.

Inspect denominators, aggregation, uncertainty, statistical versus practical differences and alternative-valid answers. Do not call an inconclusive difference proven equal or transfer one significant metric to another. Sampling, contamination, oracle access and judge calibration can change interpretation.

## Interpret an ablation

State the exact change between arms. Check whether model, tools, sources, budget, indexing, prompts, number of questions, retries and human rescue were controlled. Removing “agent” while also removing citation traversal cannot isolate adaptivity. A higher source count may confound a claimed benefit from perspective-taking.

Include setup/indexing and failed/repaired attempts in cost where reported; online-only cost is not total effort. If telemetry or methods are unavailable, say unknown. A tiny pilot can show failure modes or stability; it is not universal superiority or a performance guarantee.

## Use the result proportionally

Extract the observed result under its conditions, then explain the inferential bridge to a candidate mechanism. A useful transfer statement is: “This setting suggests follow-up after reading may improve coverage; applicability to this repository task requires a matched audition.” Do not write “this method is best” from unrelated datasets.

Primary precedents available in the supporting research include Gao et al., ALCE, arXiv `2305.14627v1` (correctness and citation support); STORM, `2402.14207v1` (question-generation ablations); PaperQA2, `2409.13740v1` (scientific QA and access limitations); and Agentless, `2407.01489v2` (repair localization). These are identifiers for applicable source retrieval, not embedded evidence or measured results for this role. Reopen the exact source before making a new numerical claim.

## Preserve disagreement and closure

Record the extracted result, inference, conditions and contrary source separately using [corpus](../../jimmy-discovery/references/corpus.md). For a literature coverage claim follow [evidence/literature](../methods/evidence-literature.md); critical reading alone is not a systematic search.

Close with what the study supports, what it does not, and the smallest target-specific observation needed. Do not run unqualified experiments or alter evaluator/oracle material. Recommendations remain hypotheses until actual independent target evidence supports them.
