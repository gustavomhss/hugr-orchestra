# Review evidence with an honest design

Read for published evidence, literature, efficacy or the distribution of studies. A quick fact fetch is not a rapid review; deep effort is not a systematic review. Do not use methodological labels as quality certificates.

## Select the design

Use the reader's question, population/context, concept/intervention, outcomes, source access and effort constraints. Ask whether the goal is understanding selected evidence, mapping a field or answering a defined evidence question with reproducible selection.

- **Narrative critical review:** reason across explicitly bounded selected material. Explain selection and limits without claiming exhaustive retrieval.
- **Scoping/mapping review:** map concepts, study types and evidence distribution. Define population/concept/context, eligibility and charting consistent with that aim. Mapping gaps do not alone establish intervention efficacy or market opportunity.
- **Systematic review:** a defined question with explicit search/selection, eligibility, extraction, appropriate appraisal and synthesis. The claim requires the actual method and access, not a headings checklist.
- **Rapid review:** explicitly abbreviate a review method for time constraints and describe which steps were shortened and how that limits conclusions. It is not a synonym for brief output.

If the requested design cannot be performed with current sources/capabilities, report the limitation and deliver an honestly named bounded review. Do not silently relabel it systematic.

## Procedure

1. Define question, eligibility, source universe, date/version window and planned synthesis before searching. Keep amendments visible with their reason rather than choosing criteria after seeing results.
2. Use exposed search/bibliographic capabilities and reference/citation traversal as available. Record actual queries, source/database, date, filters and reachable scope proportional to the promised coverage. A web search does not silently represent every literature database.
3. Distinguish duplicate reports of one study, excluded material with reasons, inaccessible plausible studies and material not examined. Preserve actual failures. Do not claim a screened count without a retained denominator.
4. Extract question/design, sample/context, intervention/comparator, outcomes, relevant effect/uncertainty, limitations and exact passages. Mark full-text, abstract-only or secondary access. Read [study reading](../references/study-reading.md) for scientific interpretation and ablations.
5. Appraise limitations appropriate to design and question. Disagreement can arise from methods, populations, outcomes, time or bias; trace these rather than vote-counting positive findings. Do not pool incompatible estimates or invent a meta-analysis.
6. Synthesize by the reader's questions and comparable themes. Explain which conclusions survive limitations, which conflict and where evidence is missing. Missing evidence is not evidence of no effect.

## Recording, tools and failure

Use admitted captures, native findings/relations and documents; preserve material search/selection/extraction details in the published review or proportional companion document body, not invented tool fields or a separate database. Native [record shapes](../../jimmy-discovery/references/record-tools.md) remain unchanged.

Unsupported PDF/binary parsing, database access, required independent screening or inaccessible full text is an explicit capability/method limit. Do not install adapters, delegate yourself or bypass denial. Give Maestro the missing source/access/evaluator need and proceed only with independent permitted evidence.

## Writing and closure

Use genre `evidence-review` and [evidence-review writing](../writing/evidence-review.md). Name the method actually performed and its search/selection limits. PRISMA 2020 is reporting guidance for systematic reviews, not proof of correct conduct or agent efficacy; Munn et al. (2018) and JBI scoping guidance distinguish review purposes.

Stop when the planned bounded selection/extraction/synthesis is complete, an indispensable source/method step is unavailable, or effort ends. Preserve contraries and decisive applicability limits in both summary and body; report [partial/blocked sufficiency](../../jimmy-discovery/references/closure.md) when the requested evidence question remains unresolved.
