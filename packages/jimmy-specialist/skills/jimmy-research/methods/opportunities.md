# Discover evidenced opportunities

Read when the task asks where meaningful improvement or new value might exist. Do not use novelty, a new combination or an unoccupied category as evidence of demand. A supplied concrete solution's fit uses [applied](applied.md); a broad territory without a value question uses [exploratory](exploratory.md).

## Inputs and discriminating questions

Use the population/workflow, desired outcome, current alternatives, observed constraints and allowed sources. A broad opportunity brief can be sharpened through early reading. Ask: whose problem is evidenced, what do they do now, which unmet part persists under those conditions, and what would disprove the value hypothesis?

## Procedure

1. Establish an evidenced need or costly failure in a bounded context. Separate measured/observed need, a stakeholder report and a speculative interest. Attribute each honestly.
2. Examine existing solutions, workarounds and the no-change alternative. Determine why the need remains: capability, access, cost, workflow, reliability or another evidenced condition. Failure of one candidate does not establish an empty market.
3. Connect a promising mechanism to the unmet part and its transfer conditions. Use [comparative](comparative.md) or [applied](applied.md) only for a material subquestion. A research benchmark can motivate a mechanism without validating customer value.
4. Record the gap as scoped to examined evidence. Retain contrary examples where a current alternative already meets the need or the proposed mechanism fails. Do not claim universal novelty/absence from web retrieval or top-K results.
5. Form a falsifiable value hypothesis: for this group/scenario, this mechanism may improve this outcome because of this evidenced connection, provided these assumptions hold. Identify the weakest decisive assumption, not a giant backlog of features.
6. Recommend the smallest next validation with measurable outcome/discard criteria and qualified executor: a bounded observation, interview, data comparison or scratch probe within a separately authorized mission. A proposal is not admitted demand or a measured result.

## Tools, records and failures

Use permitted primary-source acquisition, supplied observations, verified context and native findings/documents. Retain material need/alternative/transfer propositions with conditions and contraries through [corpus](../../jimmy-discovery/references/corpus.md). Do not create custom Memory partitions or promote hypotheses to shared Knowledge.

Missing user/workflow evidence limits demand claims. Missing alternative coverage limits gap/novelty claims. A denied source or verification hold remains a verbatim blocker; do not contact stakeholders, delegate, install/run software or publish outward to fill the gap. Maestro owns scope and executor selection.

## Writing and closure

Use genre `opportunity` and [opportunity writing](../writing/opportunity.md). Present the evidenced problem, present alternatives, bounded gap, plausible mechanism, conditions, contrary evidence and next test as one argument. Do not pad it with an ideation list or label an unsupported total addressable market.

Stop with an actionable conditional hypothesis and discard criterion, a refuted opportunity premise, an essential unavailable observation or the effort bound. A useful negative result can redirect work to the baseline. Follow [closure](../../jimmy-discovery/references/closure.md) without claiming validated demand, approved roadmap or completed implementation.
