# Analyze mechanisms and arguments

Read when the question asks why, how, under what conditions, or whether a conclusion follows from its premises. Do not equate long source summaries with analysis. Simple rules use [simple fact](simple-fact.md); an observed software incident can use [investigate](../../jimmy-discovery/methods/investigate.md).

## Inputs and discriminators

Use the exact claim/question, scenario, applicable source versions, supplied observations and intended consequence. Ask: which premises are necessary, which bridge is inferential, and what rival mechanism would predict a different observation? Identify ambiguous definitions before comparing claims.

## Procedure

1. Decompose the central question into only the premises/mechanisms needed to answer it. Establish the current baseline and scope; do not invent a full hypothesis catalog for an already settled subquestion.
2. Read primary support for each premise and its material condition. Separate statements of intention/contract from implementation and observed behavior. Follow a dependency when it could invalidate the conclusion.
3. Form a small set of materially competing explanations. For each, identify expected support, contrary evidence and the smallest discriminator. Look for a boundary case where the proposed mechanism should stop applying.
4. Record propositions and explicit inference reasons through [corpus](../../jimmy-discovery/references/corpus.md). Use `depends-on`, `supports` or `refutes` with exact revisions and reasons. Similar wording or multiple summaries of one origin are not independent premises.
5. Compare explanatory reach under compatible conditions. Narrow a conclusion when contrary evidence identifies a protected alternate path, different version or changed assumption. Keep unresolved rivals visible instead of resolving them through prose confidence.

## Tools and failure

Use permitted source acquisition, verified context, configured semantic reads for code and native record/view operations. For empirical claims or benchmark mechanisms, read [study reading](../references/study-reading.md). For technical transfer, load the conditional mechanism guide, not every stack reference.

When static reading cannot decide causality, return a qualified executor probe with fixed inputs/environment, rival outcomes, controls and effect limits. No arbitrary shell, own delegation, product edits or self-approval. A denial/hold is returned verbatim; missing observations stay missing.

## Writing and closure

Use genre `explanation` and [explanation writing](../writing/explanation.md). Organize by reasons/mechanism rather than paper/vendor order. State the supported central answer, its conditions, the bridge from evidence, material contrary evidence and unresolved alternative. An implication is conditional on the premises, not an observed result.

Deep depth iterates this method over material dependencies; it does not become a systematic literature review. Stop when the claim is sustained/refuted within scope, a rival requires unavailable decisive evidence, or effort ends. Deliver useful subanswers and the discriminator with [truthful sufficiency](../../jimmy-discovery/references/closure.md).
