# A recoverable question corpus

Read when findings need confrontation, linkage, revision, reuse or continuation. Pair with [record operations](record-tools.md); a one-source fact can retain its evidence without this setup.

## Organize around the question

The native durable ledger is authoritative for research records; managed source/document artifacts retain the bytes. Tool metadata and Markdown are projections, not a second database or a substitute archive. Do not write a local dossier, custom memory store or reasoning transcript.

Create a question record when scope, material reframing, links or continuation warrant it. Keep the current answer, important findings, live alternatives and next discriminator recoverable through records. Record material analytical state, not every sentence or every search result. A failed approach belongs in coverage or a warranted checkpoint when repeating it would waste work.

Write findings as propositions, not topic labels: “Admission reconciles the same identity, but the supplied source does not establish external effect uniqueness.” Use `nature`, `conditions`, `evidence`, `contrary` and, for a non-obvious inference or revision, `reason`. Conditions include scenario/version and assumptions that limit the conclusion. Preserve contrary evidence beside support instead of hiding it in a detached bibliography.

## Link reasons, not just mentions

Relations address exact `RecordRef` revisions with a reason:

- `supports`: the source proposition supplies a premise for the target; state how.
- `refutes`: the source conflicts with a target proposition under comparable conditions.
- `depends-on`: the conclusion requires a target premise to remain applicable.
- `related`: useful navigation without asserting support.
- `answers`: a finding addresses a question within its scope.

Do not invent relation values such as `rebuts`, `dependsOn` or `question`. Cycles may aid navigation; they do not create corroboration. Relations are separate operations, not nested finding fields.

## Revise without erasing

Host-minted identity persists when wording narrows or a material qualifier changes. Use the existing `id`, current `baseRevision` and a reason explaining the change. A distinct proposition gets a new finding. Historical relations continue to address their original revisions; update present analysis explicitly rather than allowing links to float.

On `conflict`, reopen the committed record with `research_view`, inspect the intervening change, then decide whether a new revision is warranted. Do not overwrite another writer, guess the next revision or automatically retry stale content.

## Resume and reuse honestly

On an authorized exact logical resume, reopen committed records and decisive retained sources. Recover contraries and failed approaches before extending the answer. Recheck changed environment, version and scope. Memory checkpoint text is a continuity aid, not proof that its source bytes still exist or its conclusion remains current.

Cross-task reuse requires authorized access and a currentness assessment. Link the original admitted revision and preserve its scope; do not copy a conclusion into fresh “knowledge”. Missing artifacts make their recovery unavailable. Refetching a current URL cannot reconstruct a lost historical capture.

## Turn findings into a document

Select only findings needed for the reader's question. Explain the evidence-to-conclusion bridges using [synthesis](../../jimmy-research/writing/synthesis.md), with retained qualifiers and contrary evidence. New claims discovered while writing return to source/finding checks. Publish the exact text and citation map through native document recording; a promised document must exist before [closure](closure.md).
