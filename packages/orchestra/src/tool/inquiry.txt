Run this dispatch's investigation. The host keeps the state and shows a snapshot before every turn.

Actions:
- open: question, 1-6 subquestions, profile. Once, first.
- hypothesize: a proposition to test under a subquestion.
- claim: a proposition answering one subquestion; nature extraction, observation or inference; evidence {source, lines:{start,end}}, {source, quote} or {source, pointer}; scope "universal" for only/always/never claims.
- resolve: confirm or refute a hypothesis with evidence.
- drop: abandon a hypothesis or claim, or declare a subquestion's gap, with a reason.
- check, close (answered, partial, blocked), status.

Lines: original-file, 1-based, inclusive.
Empty: zero claims, or no resolved evidence, is never answered.
Retains: every write, in the host's records; the host builds the card.
Fails: inquiry_state, binding_missing.
