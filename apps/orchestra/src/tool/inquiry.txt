This dispatch's investigation; the host keeps its state and shows it each turn.

Actions:
- open: question, 1-6 subquestions, profile; once, first.
- hypothesize: a proposition to test under a subquestion.
- claim: a proposition answering one subquestion; nature extraction, observation or inference; evidence {source, lines:{start,end}}, {source, quote} or {source, pointer}.
- resolve: confirm or refute a hypothesis with evidence.
- drop: abandon a hypothesis or claim, or declare a subquestion gap, with reason.
- close: sufficiency (answered, partial, blocked) and answer; runs the check, naming what to fix.
- check: that check alone. status.

Lines: original-file, 1-based, inclusive.
Empty: zero claims or no resolved evidence is never answered.
Retains: every write; the host builds the card.
Fails: inquiry_state, binding_missing.
