## Contract

- **Axioms:** GO-A1..7. Judge the frozen work, not a newly expanded ideal deliverable.
- **Invariants:** GO-I1..8; no producer judges itself. At most TWO campaign judge sessions exist and
  at most two judge requests run concurrently. Reuse those same sessions across every task/layer.
- **Completeness:** all four outputs covering six evidence fronts, consolidation, required criteria, conflicts and gaps are examined
  against actual supplied inputs. Persistent memory is context, never evidence of current acceptance.
- **Success:** a next-layer release is supported by exact, reproducible decisions about its full prerequisite.
- **Quality:** cite specific defect/evidence/criterion; distinguish critical failures from optional suggestions.
- **DoD:** immutable judgment receipt for the exact reviewed manifest; all required criteria PASS,
  relevant disagreements resolved, lead release recorded. A judge does not publish or self-release the layer.

## Session lifecycle and local persistence

Reserve seats `terra-1` and `terra-2`. Create each at most once when actual judging is ready; immediately
record its returned session ID, model/variant, campaign ID and local journal location before further work.
Every later request resumes that ID. Never create per-task, per-output or per-layer judge sessions.
No IDs are fabricated here. The selected runtime must support resumable sessions and durable transcripts;
if it does not, HOLD rather than substitute a fresh stateless call labelled persistent.
If one session is unavailable, use the other only where its frozen scope suffices; no silent replacement.
Loss of required session/history blocks judging until recovered or the owner explicitly amends the protocol.

Persist packet and response bodies, hashes, source/prior-layer manifest, physical-attempt receipts, actual
usage, unresolved requests, session IDs and criterion verdicts locally outside serving Atlas state. No secrets
or raw credentials in the journal. A resumed judge receives the exact current inputs and prior decision IDs;
never rely on conversational recollection in place of source/version binding. No automatic retry/refinement loop.

## Exact judgment packet and response

Packet: campaign/layer/revision; frozen source and prior-layer identities; four original outputs and digests;
consolidated manifest/body; applicable criteria and permitted unknowns; source excerpts/receipts required
to check claims; previous decisions for this exact scope; explicit request for this judge's responsibility.
No whole-repo discovery, hidden answer rewriting or global source dumps beyond the frozen evidence pack.

Response: `PASS | FIX-FIRST | HOLD`; reviewed manifest ID; one row per assigned criterion with verdict,
output section/evidence address and concise reason; critical gaps/conflicts; permitted unknowns; optional
suggestions separately nonblocking. Unsupported required content fails even if headings/hashes are correct.
Do not disclose private reasoning. Lead mechanically binds receipts to actual inputs rather than trusting
model-authored digests. Incomplete or unbound judgments do not count as approval.

## Complete-layer barrier

1. Collect/persist all four outputs. A failure does not trigger the next layer or rerun successful siblings.
2. Consolidate and check exact source/criterion coverage, references, authority labels and conflict inventory.
3. Send that SAME version to the two persistent judges, no more than two in flight.
4. PASS requires both assigned reviews to pass and no unresolved critical disagreement. A judge's
   optional suggestion cannot expand the frozen DoD. A material disagreement is HOLD, not a third judge.
5. For a justified correction, preserve the original; record a bounded amendment and re-review through
   the SAME sessions. A changed consolidation invalidates judgments bound only to its previous version.
6. Lead records accepted manifest with exact four outputs, consolidation, judgments, source/prior-layer
   bindings and explicit scope. Only then prepare/dispatch the next layer's four complementary jobs.

This is a campaign protocol, not a claim that a scheduler, persistence store or budget gate has been
implemented. Before launch verify actual session reuse, local capture, concurrency and physical-attempt
accounting; unresolved runtime guarantees remain blockers, not prose-enforced capabilities.
