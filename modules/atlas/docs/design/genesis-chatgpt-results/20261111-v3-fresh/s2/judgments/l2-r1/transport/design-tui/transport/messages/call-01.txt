## Browser UI ownership, lifecycle and accessibility

`packages/ui` supplies the Solid library; both component generations remain current, and app-shell/session-ui add higher layers. Its declared package boundary has no workspace dependencies. Representative Kobalte-based controls supply primitive behavior and styling hooks while callers supply meaningful content and labels.
