### Localization and desktop themes

UI policy requires keys for all production-visible copy, including accessibility labels, and preserves English source copy. Runtime accepts locale and optional layoutLocale independently, templates parameters and caches plural rules with a 32-entry bound. English is the fallback; missing template values become empty when substitution runs. The 63 i18n files include locale and shared-copy modules, not 63 independently validated languages. DesktopTheme carries ID/name and light/dark variants containing seeds or palettes plus overrides. Resolvers produce both generations of CSS tokens.
