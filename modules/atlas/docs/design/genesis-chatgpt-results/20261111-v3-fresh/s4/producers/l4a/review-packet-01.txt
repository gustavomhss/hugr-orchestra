```sh
bun scripts/genesis-native.ts index "$S4_REPO" entry-index.json
bun scripts/genesis-native.ts read "$S4_REPO" entry-read.json
```

`entry-index.json`:
```json
{"scope":"atlas-continuity-rag-genesis","need":"entry"}
```

`entry-read.json`:
```json

```
