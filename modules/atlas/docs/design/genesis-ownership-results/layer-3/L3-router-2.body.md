## Integrated model
Admission normalization generates or reuses a message ID, defaults delivery to `steer`, builds an expected record, admits, re-checks equivalence, and wakes only when `resume !== false` — E-AD lines 20-20; E-PJ lines 16-16; E-EC lines 18-18. This produces three receipt provenances that must stay distinct: fresh admission publishing `PromptAdmitted`, exact retry returning the stored record, and historical reconciliation synthesizing an already-promoted row from durable `Prompted` provenance — E-AD lines 24-28, 42-42; E-PJ lines 36-40. A receipt therefore need not entail a new `PromptAdmitted` event, and historical synthesis is not fresh publication.

Two comparisons decide different obligations. Retry `equivalent` compares delivery plus session/prompt only; projection `matchesProjection` adds context JSON and epoch-millis time, and the existing-row projection path also requires equal promoted sequence — E-AD lines 26-26, 36-36; E-PJ lines 32-34. Fresh provenance uses the caller's expected record; historical provenance takes delivery from the original durable event, not the user projection that omits delivery — E-AD lines 28-28; E-PJ lines 38-38.

Delivery eligibility is conditional, not admission itself. Steers require `admitted_seq <= cutoff` captured as the latest event sequence and are ordered by admission sequence; queue selection chooses the earliest unpromoted queue row without that cutoff predicate, promotes at most one, then eligible steers — E-DL lines 20-22; E-PJ lines 27-27. Pre-promotion checks, governance, continuation/idle reevaluation, allowance counter behavior, and last-step prompt insertion before `compaction.transform` determine whether pending rows become visible and what reaches a provider request — E-DL lines 26-28.

Execution coordination is process-local: `wake` coalesces when an entry is active (`pendingWake = true`, no new work) and starts `drain(key, false)` when idle, while explicit `run` joins by awaiting `done` or starts `drain(key, true)` — E-AD lines 34-34; E-EC lines 14-18. Neither wake branch proves provider completion or synchronous execution. `resume:false` suppresses this call's wake, not eligibility to an already active drain or all other scheduling — E-AD lines 43-43; E-EC lines 14-14. Intended provider-turn allowance reset on promotion and the observed `currentStep` reset only when at least one row was promoted remain separate intent/observation — E-DL lines 26-26.
