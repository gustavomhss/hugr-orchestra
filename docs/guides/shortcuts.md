# Shortcuts

The shortcuts editor lists every keyboard shortcut in Orchestra and lets you change them. It is the **Shortcuts** section of **Settings**, and it is also open as its own page titled **Shortcuts**.

## Where it lives

Open **Settings** and choose **Shortcuts**. It works without a project, because shortcuts apply to the whole app: **Shortcuts apply to the whole app, in every project.**

Each row shows what the shortcut does, its kind (general, session, navigation, model and MCP, terminal or prompt) and its current keys. A row with no keys says **Unassigned**.

## Find a shortcut

Type in **Search shortcuts** to filter the list. If nothing matches, the page says **No shortcuts match your search.**

## Change a shortcut

1. Choose **Edit** on the row.
2. In the **Press keys** dialog, focus the **Key combination** field and press the keys you want.
3. Choose **Save**.

Nothing changes until you save: the existing assignment stays as it was, and **Cancel** discards your press.

If another shortcut already uses the keys, the dialog says **Shortcut already in use:** and names it. Press a different combination, or free the other shortcut first.

To remove a binding, press Backspace or Delete alone in the field and save. The row then reads **Unassigned**.

Some keys cannot be bound: Escape, Tab, Enter, Space, a modifier on its own, and the combinations used for editing text. Pressing them in the field does nothing. Add a modifier to Tab, such as Ctrl, to bind it.

## Reset

**Reset to defaults** puts every shortcut back to the keys Orchestra ships with.

## Limits

- Shortcuts are not per project.
- A key combination can belong to one shortcut only.
