## Source-access limits and remaining questions

The substantive prompts above are supported to the extent noted, but the original WP248 PDF and direct consolidated ePrivacy/UK legal text were not fully readable through the available fetcher. Independent direct verification of those full documents, current amendments and applicable local rules remains a source-access blocker for any stronger claim. The leaf does not assert that any of these laws applies to a particular change or that a screening indicator proves noncompliance. EDPB Guidelines 2/2023, the EU Data Act and tool-license or benchmark questions also appear in A9's broader not-examined list; they are not among the privacy leaf gaps named in design §14, and no claims about them are introduced here. A10/phase-7 missing texts are separate work.
